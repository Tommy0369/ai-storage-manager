# AI Storage Manager — Store metadata draft (en)

**Product:** AI Storage Manager  
**Channel:** Direct macOS distribution (prepared copy only — do not auto-submit)

## Subtitle
See where Mac storage went. Decide safely. Verify what happened.

## Short description
AI Storage Manager maps what’s using space on your Mac, explains what it is, and only acts through Fresh Preflight → Approval → Permit → Executor → PostVerify. Protected data counts as success — not failure.

## Long description
Your Mac fills up with build output, AI models, app state, and backups. Size alone doesn’t tell you what’s safe to touch.

AI Storage Manager helps you:

1. **See** — a visual map of storage by meaning, not just folders  
2. **Understand** — what an item is, why it’s large, and what evidence supports that  
3. **Decide** — Keep, Protected, Needs Verification, or Ready — from evidence, not guesses  
4. **Act** — only after Fresh Preflight and your explicit approval  
5. **Verify** — confirmed local recovery after actions, separate from disk-free estimates  

Verified recovery is measured after the action. Moving items to Trash does not instantly free space until Trash is emptied outside this app. Vendor-native cleanup (Ollama, Hugging Face) removes exact local targets when eligible.

## Key benefits
- Visual storage map with Structure / Meaning / Decision lenses  
- Safety decisions grounded in evidence  
- Verified recovery totals after successful actions  
- Protects active work (Cursor, Voice Memos, Chrome site state, Claude runtime)  
- Offline localization for nine languages  

## Safety / privacy
- No automatic whole-Mac cleanup  
- No “always safe to delete” claims  
- Does not claim cloud backup or guaranteed recoverability without evidence  
- Full Disk Access improves visibility; limited access still runs safely  

## Platform truth
- macOS 13.0+  
- Apple silicon (arm64) for the current release artifact  

## Do not claim
- Automatic cleaner / one-click junk cleanup  
- Cleans every app  
- Free X GB without evidence  
- Universal Intel + Apple silicon unless proven for that build  
- Cloud backup service
